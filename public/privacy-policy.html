CatWorld Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

CatWorld ("we", "our", or "the app") is a mobile application designed to help cat owners manage and organize information related to their cats, including care, health records, reminders, nutrition, and other cat-related information.

This Privacy Policy explains what information CatWorld collects, how that information is used, how it is stored, and how users can request deletion of their account and associated data.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, CatWorld may collect your name, email address, and Firebase user identifier (UID) for account management and authentication.

Cat and Pet Information

You may voluntarily provide information about your cat, such as its name, breed, age, weight, health records, medications, reminders, and other care information.

Photos

CatWorld may request access to your device camera so you can take photos of your cat. Photos used for a cat profile are stored in the app's private storage on your device and are not uploaded to our backend as part of this feature.

Location Information

CatWorld may request approximate or precise location permission when you use the Emergency Vet feature. Location is used to help you search for nearby veterinary clinics through Google Maps.

CatWorld does not intentionally store your location on its remote servers.

AI Assistant Information

When you use the AI Cat Assistant, information contained in your request and relevant cat profile context may be transmitted to Google's Generative Language API to generate a response.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage your CatWorld account.
  • Store and synchronize your cat information.
  • Provide cat care and health-related features.
  • Provide reminders and other app functionality.
  • Help you search for nearby veterinary services.
  • Provide responses through the AI Cat Assistant.
  • Provide account deletion and data management functionality.

3. Data Storage

Account and cat information may be stored using Firebase Authentication and Firebase Firestore. Data transmitted between the application and these services is protected in transit using HTTPS/TLS.

Cat photos used by the application are stored in the application's private storage on the device unless a feature explicitly states otherwise.

4. Third-Party Services

CatWorld uses third-party services to provide certain functionality, including:

  • Firebase Authentication for account authentication.
  • Firebase Firestore for cloud data storage
